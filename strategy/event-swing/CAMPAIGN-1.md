# event-swing, campaign 1: earnings events on basket A (pre-registered; simulation only)

**Status: FROZEN.** Written 2026-11-06 on the owner's order of 2026-11-06 (EXPERIMENT-LOG.md entry 1), before any event row, filing document or release text existed on this machine and before any strategy outcome was computed. Pinned by LF-normalised SHA-256 in `server/eventswing/config.js` (`PREREG`) together with `classifier-v1.json` (the word lists of section 4) and `matrix-v1.json` (the configurations of section 5). A change is a new campaign document with a log entry naming every result seen; this file is never edited.

Every number below is an **author-selected experimental assumption**, chosen before any result and explained where it is set; none is the owner's trading preference. Simulation only: no capital, broker, order, paper-trading or account code.

## 1. Hypotheses and what each comparison tests

| Family | Hypothesis (to test, not to assume) | The comparison that tests it |
|---|---|---|
| A. Pre-earnings risk management | Selling a holding shortly before an expected earnings announcement and buying it back once the announcement is known improves the result of holding through it, after costs and missed upside. | A1, A2 against the hold baseline A0 and against the placebo (section 9): is being out around *estimated announcement dates* better than being out for equally long spells at random non-announcement dates? |
| B. Post-earnings recovery | After a negative reaction to an announcement, a defined recovery close identifies a rebound worth buying; guidance that is unchanged or raised separates good rebounds from bad ones. | B1 against its family baseline B0 (buy the reaction at once) and its price-only control BP (the same recovery rule after any two-session fall); B2 (guidance unchanged or raised) against B3 (guidance lowered); every one against the placebo. |
| C. Positive continuation | A positive reaction to an announcement, especially with raised guidance, continues over the following weeks. | C1 against C0 (buy after every announcement) and CP (the same rule after any two-session rise); C2 (raised guidance) against C1; the placebo. |
| D. Negative-news avoidance | Skipping an underlying entry rule's entries for a while after lowered guidance or an adverse reaction improves that rule. | D1 (after lowered guidance), D2 (after a reaction of −5% or worse) and D3 (after any announcement, the calendar control) against the underlying rule D0; the placebo. |

**Labels.** A1, A2, B0, B1, C0, C1, D2, D3 use only announcement timing and prices: they are **event-calendar strategies**. B2, B3, C2, D1 also use the release's explicit guidance words (section 4): they are the **news-understanding** configurations, in the narrow mechanical sense of section 4 and no other. BP, CP and D0 are **price-only**. A0 is the hold. Whether news information adds anything is answered only by B2 against B1 and BP, C2 against C1 and CP, and D1 against D2, D3 and D0.

**Unsupported (no data, so no configuration):** results against contemporaneous expectations (no free historical consensus); reported figures as known at the announcement (XBRL comes later and may be restated); a pre-announcement calendar as known beforehand (paid; free calendars show realised dates); elections and policy announcements (a separate future family). ## 3. Events, exactly

**3.1 Records.** The event source is SEC EDGAR: every 8-K and 8-K/A filed by the stock's pinned registrant (DATA-INVENTORY.md section 3; never a predecessor) and accepted from 2015-01-01 to 2021-12-31, Eastern time, whose item list includes 2.02. From each filing: the accession number, the form, the acceptance date-time (Eastern, to the second), the report date ("period of report"), the filing date, the items and the exhibit-99 document (types EX-99.1, EX-99.01, EX-99, EX-99.(1), EX-99.A, in that order of preference, the first in document order). Nothing else is used. A filing whose acceptance date-time or report date cannot be read is recorded as unparsed and stops the download (it is never defaulted).

**3.2 Grouping into events.** Per stock, the original 8-K filings with item 2.02 are taken in order of acceptance; a filing joins the current event if its report date is at most 3 calendar days after that event's first report date, otherwise it starts a new event. An 8-K/A is attached to the event whose first report date is within 3 calendar days of its own report date and otherwise recorded as "amendment only" and excluded; an amendment never changes an event's dates. For each event: **D**, its earliest report date; **A**, its earliest acceptance; its release text, the exhibit 99 of its earliest filing that has one. A report date after the acceptance's Eastern date is an error in the record: D is then the acceptance's date and the event is flagged. **Interim events:** an event whose D is less than 45 days after the stock's previous event's D is marked interim (a mid-quarter update or a second filing); it is an event like any other for families B to D, and it creates no estimate in family A.

**3.3 Availability: when the event may be used.** The decision of session t is taken at that session's close (16:00 Eastern, 13:00 on early-close days, from `server/calendar.js`), using bars completed at that close. An event may be used at the decision of session t only if A is strictly before t's close. Its **availability session** is the first NYSE session whose close is after A, moved to the stock's next session with a bar if the stock has none that day. So an 8-K accepted after the close (the usual case) is first used at the next session's close, and its earliest fill is the open after that: **an event known only after the close never receives that day's closing fill, and no fill is ever at a close.** The acceptance is an upper bound on when the release was public (the press release normally comes first), so this rule can only make an event later than the market saw it, never earlier. **Unknown acceptance time:** if a filing's acceptance time is missing, A is taken as 23:59:59 Eastern on its filing date, which delays the event by one session (the documented conservative delay). **Sensitivity:** every configuration is also run with every availability session moved one further session later (section 7).

**3.4 The reaction.** The pre-event session **p** is the stock's last session with a bar dated strictly before D (so it closed before any release on D, whatever its hour). The reaction **R = C(availability) / C(p) − 1**, with C(p) converted to post-split units if a split falls after p. It spans at least one full session of trading after the acceptance. Classes: **negative** R ≤ −0.05, **positive** R ≥ +0.05, otherwise **neutral**. The 5% threshold is an author-selected assumption (large enough to be an announcement-driven move for these large companies, small enough to occur in a usable share of events). **Release-time class** (reported, never a trigger): before the open (A's date is D, A before 09:30), during the session (09:30 to the close), after the close, filed after its report date (A's date later than D: the release hour on D is unknown), non-session day, unknown (no acceptance time).

**3.5 Exclusions.** An event is excluded and counted by reason if D is before the stock's listing date, it has no pre-event bar, its availability session is after 2021-12-31, or it is amendment only. 2015 events are used only to build family A's estimates.

**3.6 Family A's estimated dates (never the realised date).** Each non-interim event E creates one estimate **Ê = D(E) + 364 days** (52 weeks: the same weekday a year later), known from the close of E's availability session (for 2015 events, before the window starts). An estimate within 45 days of an earlier estimate of the same stock is dropped. The **target session** e is the first NYSE session on or after Ê. For reporting only, the estimate's **actual** is the first event of the stock with D within 30 days either side of Ê ("matched"); if none, the estimate is "unmatched". A stock's first-year announcements have no estimate when no same-registrant event exists a year earlier (Alphabet before Q3 2016, PayPal before Q2 2016, Vistra and Snap in their first listed year): those are held through and counted as "no estimate".

## 4. The guidance classifier (mechanical; fixed before any release text was downloaded)

Applied by code (`server/eventswing/events.js` `classifyRelease`) exactly as written here and in `classifier-v1.json`, never by a person or a language model; nobody reads a release to check or correct it. It is crude on purpose, and its crudeness is a limitation of every news-understanding result.

1. **Text.** The event's exhibit 99 as filed (HTML or text). No exhibit, or no text after step 2: class **no_text**.
2. **Normalise.** Remove `<script>` and `<style>` blocks; replace the tags `br`, `p`, `div`, `tr`, `li`, `td`, `th`, `table`, `h1` to `h6` (opening or closing) by a newline and remove every other tag; decode the entities `&amp; &lt; &gt; &quot; &#39; &apos; &nbsp;` and every numeric entity; lower-case; turn curly quotes into straight ones and every dash character into `-`; collapse runs of spaces and tabs to one space.
3. **Sentences.** Split at every newline and after every `.`, `!`, `?` or `;` that is followed by whitespace. Drop a sentence that contains any **excluded phrase**: `forward-looking`, `forward looking`, `differ materially`, `safe harbor`, `risks and uncertainties`, `private securities litigation reform act`, `undue reliance`.
4. **Words.** A sentence's words are its maximal runs of the letters a to z.
5. **Counts.** A sentence counts as a *raise* sentence if it holds a **guidance noun** and a **raise word**, a *lower* sentence if it holds a guidance noun and a **lower word**, a *reaffirm* sentence if it holds a guidance noun and a **reaffirm word** (one sentence can count in several).
   - Guidance nouns: `guidance`, `outlook`, `forecast`, `forecasts`.
   - Raise words: `raise`, `raises`, `raised`, `raising`, `boost`, `boosts`, `boosted`, `boosting`, `lift`, `lifts`, `lifted`, `lifting`, `increases`, `increased`, `increasing`.
   - Lower words: `lower`, `lowers`, `lowered`, `lowering`, `reduce`, `reduces`, `reduced`, `reducing`, `cut`, `cuts`, `cutting`, `decreases`, `decreased`, `decreasing`, `withdraw`, `withdraws`, `withdrew`, `withdrawn`, `withdrawing`, `suspend`, `suspends`, `suspended`, `suspending`.
   - Reaffirm words: `reaffirm`, `reaffirms`, `reaffirmed`, `reaffirming`, `reiterate`, `reiterates`, `reiterated`, `reiterating`, `maintain`, `maintains`, `maintained`, `maintaining`.
6. **Class.** r, l, a = the numbers of raise, lower and reaffirm sentences. **lowered** if l > 0 and r = 0; **raised** if r > 0 and l = 0; **mixed** if both; **reaffirmed** if r = l = 0 and a > 0; **none_detected** otherwise.

Groupings used by the configurations: *unchanged or raised* = {raised, reaffirmed}; *deteriorating* = {lowered}; mixed, none_detected and no_text belong to neither. Known weaknesses, accepted in advance: "lower" and "increased" also occur as adjectives and in sentences about results rather than guidance; withdrawing guidance (2020) counts as lowered; a company that never gives guidance is none_detected; negation is ignored. The counts r, l, a are stored with each event so a reader can see what drove the class without reading the text.

Why these settings (assumptions, before any result): the 10-session watch and the 20-session holding period cover the few weeks in which post-announcement drift is usually described; the recovery close (a close above the previous high and above the previous close) is the plainest definition of a turn; L* and C(p) are the natural invalidation levels of "the low held" and "the reaction was undone"; the two-session window of the price-only controls matches the reaction window of the usual after-close announcement (p is the session before D, the availability session the one after); the 20-session pause in family D matches the holding horizon of families B and C; D0 is campaign 3's M1 n=3 because it is the project's best-documented price rule on these bars (its prospective journal is separate and is not touched). Interim events take part in families B to D like any other event.

## 6. Rules common to every configuration

- **Inputs and when they become available:** bars at their session's close; an event at its availability session (section 3.3), with R from closes up to that session; the guidance class with the event (the release is part of the filing); family A's estimates at the availability session of the event that creates them; dividends and splits on their ex-dates as stored.
- **Overnight announcements:** an announcement after the close (or before the next open) reaches the decision of the next session; the fill is the open after that, gap included.
- **Holidays and early closes:** sessions and close times come from the NYSE calendar (`server/calendar.js`); a session without a bar for a stock does not count for that stock's watches, time exits or windows.
- **Gaps:** fills happen at the open, whatever the gap; a buy whose fill is at or below S is rejected and recorded; an invalidation or time exit sells at the next open, gap included.
- **Re-entry:** after any exit, the stock can be entered again by the next trigger; there is no cooling-off period beyond the rules above.
- **Overlapping events and multiple signals on one stock:** one position per stock, no adding; a trigger while the stock is held or has a pending order is ignored (recorded "already_held"); in B a new trigger during a watch restarts the watch from the new trigger (recorded); in D a pause starting during another pause extends it to 20 sessions after the later event.
- **Shared cash and priority:** all signals of one close are sized together with equal shares of the available cash and risk room (campaign 3); orders fill at the next open, sells before buys, stocks in the basket's listed order; no signal has priority over another.
- **Missing data:** an event that cannot be placed (section 3.5) is excluded and counted; a stock without a bar at a decision has no decision that day; a buy whose fill session has no bar is cancelled; a sell waits for the stock's next bar; a release with no exhibit is no_text (excluded from B2, B3, C2 and D1's pauses, included everywhere else). No missing event is filled in and no expectation is invented.

## 7. Sensitivities (not configurations; never selected on)

Every configuration is also run (i) with **double trading costs** (half-spread and slippage both doubled; fees unchanged) and (ii) with **every event one session later** than section 3.3 allows (family A's estimates unchanged; its re-entries one session later). They are evaluations of the same rule under harsher execution, reported beside it, and they carry no inference.

## 8. Reporting (every run)

Net profit in US$, total and annualised return, maximum drawdown, average invested fraction and time in cash, completed trades, holding periods, turnover, costs; net per calendar year and per stock; gross-gain concentration (the top stock's share of the summed positive stock P/L); results by event category (reaction class, guidance class, release-time class, interim or not) with the excluded events by reason; signals skipped, cancelled, rejected or reduced, and their missed outcome (the stock's move from the next open to the close of the planned holding period); for family A every estimate's outcome: exited before the actual, exited on its report date, held through because the actual came earlier, out more than 10 sessions before it, unmatched, no estimate; for each exit spell whether holding would have won because the stock rose, and the spell's dollar effect e = shares × (exit fill − re-entry fill); for family B every trigger's fate: no recovery within the watch (with the stock's move from the trigger close to 20 sessions later), entered and invalidated, entered and timed out with a loss, entered and gained; the two sensitivities; against (a) the index, (c) the exposure-matched hold, the family baseline and the matched price-only control, with the exposure part (the difference between the two runs' exposure-matched holds) and the remainder reported separately as **descriptive attribution, not evidence of timing or selection**. Chronological blocks: each calendar year 2016 to 2021 and the halves 2016-01-04 to 2018-12-31 and 2019-01-02 to 2021-12-31, reported for every run. Dependence: the number of distinct availability dates, the largest group of stocks sharing one, and the placebo's clustering (section 9).

## 9. The inferential test: a date-clustered event placebo

**What it tests.** H0 for a configuration: its gain over its own exposure-matched hold is no larger when its events sit on the real announcement dates than when the same events sit on random non-announcement dates of the same stocks. The statistic is **T = the run's net profit minus its exposure-matched hold's net profit**, so a run is not credited for merely being invested in a rising market, and a placebo run with no trades has T = 0.

**Placebo events (families B, C, D).** Every usable event of a stock is moved to a random session u of that stock; its pre-event session moves with it, keeping its distance in the stock's sessions; its guidance class travels with it; its reaction is recomputed from the prices at the new dates and the configuration's own rules are re-applied unchanged. A session u is **eligible** for the stock if no real event of the stock has its report date or availability session within 30 of the stock's sessions before or after u, the stock has at least 21 earlier closes, and u is not the last window session. **Clustering:** real events whose availability sessions fall on the same calendar date form one cluster and move together to one date drawn uniformly from the dates eligible for every stock in the cluster (if no such date exists the members are drawn separately, and the number of such clusters is reported). Within one resample, two placebo events of one stock less than 30 of its sessions apart are not allowed: a draw that breaks this is repeated (at most 1,000 times, then the resample fails and is reported).

**Placebo spells (family A).** Each real exit spell (the exit fill session x to the re-entry decision session y of one stock, length L = y − x sessions) is moved to a random start x′ with the same length; x′ is eligible if no real event of the stock has its report date or availability session within the stock's sessions x′ − 10 to x′ + L + 10, and the spell lies inside the stock's window. Spells that start on the same calendar date move together, as above. The run is re-simulated with the placebo spells forced (sell at the open of x′, re-buy at the open after y′).

**The p-value.** R = 2,000 resamples, one mulberry32 stream with seed **20261106**, consumed in a fixed order (configurations in matrix order, clusters in date order). p = (1 + #{r : T_r ≥ T_obs}) / (1 + R); the smallest attainable p is 1 / 2,001. The placebo is run for the ten configurations of the **Holm family**: A1, A2, B1, B2, B3, C1, C2, D1, D2, D3 (m = 10, fixed now; a failed, descriptive-only or trade-less member keeps its place in m and counts as not significant). Holm step-down at α = 0.05 over m = 10; raw and adjusted p are both reported. The baselines and controls (A0, B0, C0, D0, BP, CP) carry no test: they are comparators.

**What it cannot say.** It says nothing about sizing or cash constraints beyond what the re-simulation includes; a small p on these previously examined bars is a development result, not evidence of future profit; with ten stocks and about six years the test has little power.

**Descriptive only.** A configuration whose triggering category has fewer than 20 events in the inventory (B0 and B1: negative reactions; B2: negative reactions with guidance raised or reaffirmed; B3: negative reactions with guidance lowered; C1: positive reactions; C2: positive reactions with guidance raised; C0 and D3: usable events; D1: lowered guidance; D2: negative reactions; A1 and A2: estimates with a target session in the window) is run and reported as **descriptive only**: no placebo, no candidate.

## 10. Selection: a development candidate needs every condition

1. Not descriptive only; at least 30 completed trades (family A: at least 30 completed exit spells).
2. Net profit above its family baseline's and above its matched price-only control's (B1, B2, B3 against B0 and BP; C1, C2 against C0 and CP; D1, D2, D3 against D0; A1, A2 against A0, the placebo standing in for a price-only control).
3. T > 0 at the base case, at double costs and with the one-session delay.
4. Maximum drawdown at most 15% (families B, C, D, as campaign 3) or no deeper than A0's (family A, a hold overlay).
5. Best-5% stress: with the K = ceil(5% of n) best outcomes removed (family A: the best exit-spell effects), the net profit is still above the family baseline's (family A: the summed spell effects still positive).
6. Concentration: no single stock holds more than 40% of the gross gain (family A: of the summed positive per-stock spell effects).
7. Holm-adjusted placebo p at most 0.05 within the family of ten.
8. The accounting reconciles and the data checks passed.

Several candidates are reported side by side; none is picked automatically and **NONE is a valid outcome**. Ending balance alone never selects anything. A candidate is a **development candidate on previously examined prices**: it opens no evaluation window (none exists for this programme), changes nothing in any other programme, and would need the owner's separate decision (for example a prospective journal of its own) before anything else happens. The criteria are never lowered and the search is never extended because no candidate appears.

## 11. Budget and chronology

- 16 configurations, each run once at the base case and once under each sensitivity (48 runs), plus the placebo for the members of the Holm family that are not descriptive only (at most 10 × 2,000 re-simulations). Up to **six replacement runs** solely for verified defects, each with the defect, its verification and the correction as text; the broken result is kept and every attempt counts.
- **Execution cap: two hours** of measured worker time for the whole campaign (runs and placebo), checkpointed after every run; implementation time is recorded separately (the code-frozen entry's time against the campaign's first start).
- **Before the campaign can start** (the gate, `server/eventswing/campaign.js`): the pinned files match; the pre-registration commit is an ancestor of HEAD and holds them; no uncommitted change in the event-swing code or documents; the event data is complete (no unparsed filing) and its manifest hash recomputes; the `**Event inventory**` entry and one `**Code frozen for campaign 1**` entry (naming the code revision, the pre-registration commit and the data manifest hash) are in the log; the lock is free; at least 1 GB of disk is free.
- **No fitted model and no train/test split:** the classifier is a fixed word rule, every threshold is fixed here, and the whole window is one previously examined development sample reported in the blocks of section 8; nothing is tuned on any block.
- Notifications are in-app rows only, titled "development improvement—not validated", for a configuration that beats both its family baseline and its matched control; nothing is sent anywhere.

## 12. What this campaign cannot show

That earnings events are tradeable in general (one hand-picked basket of survivors); anything about expectations or results (unsupported); anything about elections or policy; anything about periods after 2021; anything about real fills, which the cost model only approximates. Results are exploratory, on prices that 151 earlier runs examined.
