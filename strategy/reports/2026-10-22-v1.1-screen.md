# cb-1.1.0 pre-holdout screen (swing) — 2026-10-22

**Outcome: cb-1.1.0 FAILS the pre-holdout screen.** Four of six conditions
fail (S1–S4). Under the conditions and the owner's limit recorded in
`PARAMETER-LOG.md` before any cb-1.1.0 result existed:

- **The holdout is not run.**
- **cb-1.0.0 closes, with no v1.2.**

The owner makes the final call. Nothing further has been run.

These are simulated results for a hypothetical US$100,000 account. No orders
were placed and the real portfolio was not read. Development and validation
were seen before this version was made, so they are in-sample here. A pass
would only have allowed the holdout; it would not have been evidence of an
edge. The failure is negative evidence: on the data already seen, the
corrected entry trades often and loses after costs.

## Step 0: the owner's arithmetic, checked before deciding

The check used only stored event counts and event details from runs 1–8. It
used no trade outcomes.

- **The counts are confirmed.** Swing validation under cb-1.0.0 had 114
  signals and these rejections:

  | Rejection | Count |
  |---|---|
  | Beyond the target | 26 |
  | Costs | 36 |
  | Size below one share | 2 |
  | Maximum entry at or below the stop | 1 |

  Intraday had the same 114 signals, plus 17 last-hour rejections.
- **One premise was corrected, and the conclusion still holds.** Candidate A's
  stop is tighter only when the breakout candle's low is at or below the
  ceiling. In 35 of the 114 signals the low was above the ceiling, so A's stop
  would have been wider there. Six of the 36 cost rejections are in that
  group. A's ceiling is therefore 114 − 26 − 30 = **at most 58 orders**, not
  about 50. That is still below the 60 trades S1 needs.
- **Candidate B's ceiling is confirmed.** It keeps the stop and the reference
  entry, so the cost rejections are unchanged. That gives at most about 76
  swing orders (114 − 36 − 2) and 60 intraday orders.
- **Actual result.** Swing validation placed **69 orders**, within that
  ceiling.

## Implementation (done before any run)

- **New config.** `configs/cb-1.1.0.json` differs from cb-1.0.0 only in
  `target`: `mode: risk_multiple`, and the box-height multiple is removed.
  cb-1.0.0's file and hash (`5167db4c…`) are unchanged.
- **Engine-1.1.0.**
  - At the signal there is no target. The maximum entry is the gap limit.
  - At the fill, target = E + 2 × (E − stop), from the actual fill price,
    rounded up to the tick.
  - cb-1.0.0's hand-checked engine tests pass unchanged.
- **New tests** in `tests/strategy-v1.1.test.js`:
  - hand-checked targets of 102.97 and 104.17;
  - the gap limit binding;
  - no look-ahead: changing bars after the open leaves the target unchanged;
  - prefix invariance and a snapshot round-trip on synthetic data.
- **Updated tests.** Existing tests that assumed cb-1.0.0 was the newest
  version now expect 1.1.0.
- **Docs.** `ENGINE-SPEC.md` §4a and `RULES.md` §4a describe the rule.
- **Test suite.** `npm test`: 283 of 283 pass.
- **The Lab's own log.** `lab_param_changes` records cb-1.1.0 with the data
  seen before the change.
