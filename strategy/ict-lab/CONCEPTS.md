# ICT Lab: concepts, one exact mechanical definition each (Part A)

**Status:** DRAFT until the Part A commit; pinned and never edited afterwards (a change is a new version with an EXPERIMENT-LOG.md entry). Every definition uses only bars closed at the decision time (PROTOCOL.md section 10). Long side only. Prices are dollars with cents, compared exactly on the stored values (the engine, in Part C, keeps integer units of 0.0001 as sweep-bos does).

**How to read a row.** Each concept is a numbered HYPOTHESIS (H1 onward, this lab's own numbering) with its origin: **owner** (the owner's specification of 2026-11-04 or the owner's sweep-bos instruction), **author** (the author's mechanical choice, not ICT's teaching), or **standard convention** (the common textbook form of an ICT term, as the owner wrote it). The "sweep-bos" column says whether the definition is retained from, changed from, or new relative to sweep-bos PRE-REGISTRATION.md (its H1 to H15; the full disposition table is PROTOCOL.md section 13). Every number is proposed, for the owner to confirm, unless marked owner.

Notation: session d has bars 0..n−1 (n = 78, or 42 on an early close); O, H, L, C of bar i are O[i], H[i], L[i], C[i]; s is the sweep bar; the WINDOW is bars with index ≤ 17 (the 10:55 bar; its close is 11:00:00).

## Decision order inside a session (so no rule reads a later bar)

At the close of each bar k = 0, 1, ..., 17 of a tradeable session, in this order: (1) update the confirmed swings (H1) with bars ≤ k; (2) if no setup is open and bar k is a sweep bar (H4), open the setup, λ = k; (3) if a setup is open, update the sweep extreme (H5); (4) test the spec's confirmation on bar k (H6 for specs 1 and 2; H10's close-through for spec 3; H13's failure and BOS coupling for spec 4); (5) on confirmation, build the zone (H9 / H11 / H10 / H13), the stop (H14), the target (H17), the entry (H15), check validity (H18, H15's entry-below-close, H14's minimum distance) and size the trade (H19); (6) place the order. Fills and exits are then evaluated on bars after k (H15, H16). A fill on bar m ends the session's entries. An order that expires at the close of bar 17 cannot re-arm anything, because the window has closed with it; an order that is cancelled earlier does not exist in round 1 (there is no cancellation rule before bar 17), so in practice the re-arm of H15 applies only when a confirmation is refused at step (5) (no zone, zone too small, entry not below the close, stop too close, unfunded) and a later bar ≤ 17 confirms again.

## What none of this uses

The previous session's 5-minute bars; pre-market or after-hours bars; the prior-session high; daily bias; any bar after the decision bar; fractional shares, leverage, shorting, margin; news, volume (bars carry it, no rule reads it), or any indicator.
