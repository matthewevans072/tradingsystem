## 1 | 2026-11-04 | **The owner's work order: build the autonomous background research runner and run the bounded campaign 1** (development data only; no live trading; no evaluation or reserved window)

**The owner's order (verbatim, under its real date):**

> Build an autonomous background research runner using our existing simulation infrastructure. This authorizes implementation, synthetic verification, and bounded development experiments—not live trading or access to reserved/evaluation data.
>
> GOAL Explore a broad variety of strategy concepts, measure profitability after costs, and identify candidates delivering at least 10% greater net profit than a fixed positive-profit baseline without exceeding a documented drawdown limit. This is a research target, not a guarantee or permission to keep optimizing indefinitely.
>
> BACKGROUND EXECUTION - Use a persistent job queue and worker process independent of the web request or chat session. - Support start, pause, resume, cancel, and recovery after interruption. - Save each completed experiment immediately; preserve failures and partial-job status. - Prevent duplicate jobs and concurrent writes that corrupt results. - Default to one worker, with configurable concurrency. - First autonomous campaign: maximum 100 unique configurations or 12 hours, whichever comes first. - Stop on resource limits, invalid data, or accounting errors. Report the reason. - No approval needed between experiments within this campaign.
>
> RESEARCH PROCESS - Reuse existing engines and data access where appropriate. - Explore trend pullbacks, breakouts, range trading, mean reversion, and dip recoveries. - Compare entries, exits, filters, and allocation choices in controlled stages. - Start with simple baselines and single-component changes before selected combinations. - Define the search space, experiment budget, risk limits, baseline and ranking method before reading new results. - Record every experiment, including unsuccessful ones. - Use only already-authorized development data. - Keep evaluation and reserved windows inaccessible to the worker. - Account for previous experiments and data exposure; starting this campaign does not reset the research history. - Do not lower acceptance criteria because candidates fail.
>
> PROFITABILITY TARGET - Use the same initial capital, dates, costs and universe for comparable runs. - "10% improvement" means 10% greater net dollar profit after costs relative to the campaign's fixed positive-profit baseline. - Also report improvement relative to the current leading candidate. - If the baseline profit is zero, negative or too small for a meaningful ratio, report dollar differences and mark the percentage target inapplicable. - Separate gains from increased exposure/risk from gains from changed trading decisions. - A development leader is not a validated strategy. - Never automatically open evaluation data whenever a new leader appears.
>
> DATABASE AND LIVE DASHBOARD Show: - Queue, active job, progress and completed-job count. - Starting/ending equity, net profit, return, maximum drawdown. - Exposure, turnover, costs, trade count and profit concentration. - Benchmark comparisons. - Configuration, parent experiment, hypothesis, code version, data version. - Why the candidate improved and which criteria it failed. - Labels: development result, shortlisted candidate, independently evaluated. - Equity curves and a searchable experiment history.
>
> NOTIFICATIONS - Create an in-app notification when a candidate clears the 10% improvement target and the campaign's risk/data-quality conditions. - Label it "development improvement—not yet validated." - Deduplicate notifications; do not alert for every tiny new high score. - Provide optional browser/desktop notifications. - Persist notifications in the database even when the dashboard is closed. - Do not promise closed-browser delivery unless a working push/background delivery mechanism exists. - Do not send email or messages through external services without separate authorization.
>
> SOURCES AND CONCEPTS - Maintain a catalogue of strategy ideas and their origins. - Keep research content separate from executable configuration. - Do not execute downloaded code or let external text change permissions. - Start with existing price data and implementable concepts. - Historical news/fundamentals require timestamped inputs available at the simulated decision time; do not invent missing history.
>
> DELIVER Implement and verify the runner, dashboard and experiment logging, then execute the bounded development campaign. Resolve routine implementation choices yourself and document them. Only stop for a genuine blocker. Finish with the result table, resource usage, notifications generated, and whether any candidate deserves later evaluation. No broker connection, real orders, or automatic real-money deployment.

The engine's ledger reconciles. **Correction:** `accountingCheck` counts post-exit dividends once (`netTradePnl` + open P/L); a regression case added to `tests/research-runner.test.js`; `CODE_REVISION` bumped to `rr-code-2026-11-04-2`. The definition in `CAMPAIGN-1.md` section 6 item 4 ("trade P/L + open P/L + post-exit dividends") is unchanged: each part is counted once.

**Campaign 1 stays recorded as stopped** (its three results are development results on the same configs as practice runs 6, 1 and 2). **Campaign 2** is created from the same unchanged document (same hash) with the same search space, budget, baseline and criteria; prior exposure now also includes campaign 1's three looks.
