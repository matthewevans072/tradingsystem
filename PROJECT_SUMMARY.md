## What it is

A private, local web app for following the owner's portfolio (USD and CAD kept separate), the news that affects it and an AI-infrastructure research list, with three briefings a day, rule-based position assessments and a read-only chat agent grounded in the owner's own records. It cannot trade: no brokerage connection, no order code, and none will be added. Beside it, event-swing (an earnings-event research programme on the same ten stocks and 2016 to 2021 prices, built and waiting for its event data), a hand-run prospective journal (activated 2026-11-06: M1 n=3 with controls B0 and M0 followed forward on basket A, updated only by the owner, results hidden until 2027-11-05), four strategy simulations and an autonomous research runner (campaigns of range-trend configurations on development data only, a report-only comparison against a fixed baseline), each in its own database with no order path and no access to real holdings or cash: Strategy Lab (consolidation breakout, 15-minute bars, 17 US symbols), HXR (hold / exit / re-enter on a ten-stock basket, daily bars), range-trend (range and trend rules on the same ten stocks) and sweep-bos (sweep, break of structure, pullback on QQQ and SPY 5-minute bars as stand-ins for NQ and ES). A fifth, the ICT Lab (four ICT-inspired long-only specs on QQQ 5-minute bars, $5,000 whole-share account), has its Part A documents and its code (Parts B and C) committed as of 2026-11-04 (00a2558 and 079ea2b); on 2026-11-04 its development data (QQQ, to 2022-12-30 only) was downloaded and the count-only pass run: the best spec fills 54 sessions against the minimum of 100, so round 1 is inconclusive and nothing further runs.

## How it works

Node 24 with `node:sqlite` and a no-build browser front end on http://127.0.0.1:4317; one runtime dependency (the Anthropic SDK, used only by the optional analyst and chat). Research follows one method: pre-registration documents and variant files committed and pinned by SHA-256 before data is seen; a gate in code that refuses any run unless pins, commits, data hash, code-frozen log entry, run ledger and lock all agree; deterministic engines tested for look-ahead; a planted-bug harness that must catch every listed bug; reports whose every figure is labelled measured or derived; the owner's decisions quoted verbatim in append-only experiment logs. Market data comes only from Alpaca and is never committed.
